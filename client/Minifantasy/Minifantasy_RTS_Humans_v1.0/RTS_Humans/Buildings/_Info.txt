- Each building has 7 versions:
	- 1 pre-construction state
	- 2 under construction states
	- 1 completed state
	- 2 Damaged states
	- 1 Destroyed state

- The buildings have different square base sizes:
	- 64x64px for the Castle
	- 48x48px for the Barracks, the Archery Range and the Stables
	- 32x32px for the Blacksmith, the farm and the field

- Possible Building Functions:
	-  Castle, the main production building for workers and basic upgrades
	- Barracks, melee units, and melee-focused upgrades
	- Archery Range, ranged units and ranged-focused upgrades
	- Stables, chivalry and chivalry-focused upgrades
	- Blacksmith, general upgrades/unit unlocks
	- Farm/fields, food production, unit limit
