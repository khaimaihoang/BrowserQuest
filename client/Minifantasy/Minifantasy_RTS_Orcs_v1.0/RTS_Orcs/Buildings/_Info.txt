- Each building has 7 versions:
	- 1 pre-construction state
	- 2 under construction states
	- 1 completed state
	- 2 Damaged states
	- 1 Destroyed state

- The buildings have different square base sizes:
	- 64x64px for the Stronghold
	- 48x48px for the Barracks, Hunters_Camp and the Warg_Pen
	- 32x32px for the Forge, the Pig_Farm and the Field

- Possible Building Functions:
	- Stronghold, the main production building for workers and basic upgrades
	- Barracks, melee units, and melee-focused upgrades
	- Hunters_Camp, ranged units and ranged-focused upgrades
	- Warg_Pen, chivalry and chivalry-focused upgrades
	- Forge, general upgrades/unit unlocks
	- Pig_Farm/fields, food production, unit limit
